SNOLAP — App Review materials

For the App Store review team. This page is not linked from the public site.

1. Sample skiing clip (for video coaching)

Save this clip to Photos on the test device, then open the Coaching tab, choose Upload video and pick it. Analysis runs entirely on the device; the video is not uploaded.

Download the sample clip (long-press the link on iPhone and choose Download, then Save Video from the Files app).

2. Screen recording (ride recording in the background)

This is a real recording made on an iPhone, indoors and away from a ski resort (the ski season had not started yet). It shows starting a recording, the location permission step, locking the phone, the lock-screen indicator while the phone stays locked for about four minutes, stopping, and the saved record. The app was recorded in Korean. Under 기록 품질 · 원본 데이터 (record quality and raw data) the record shows location received for the whole time, no gaps, and the background and return events, which is what background location is used for.

The phone did not move, so the track is a single point and the distance is 0 m. Lift and run detection needs altitude change on a ski slope, so this recording shows no runs; the run list is shown in the App Store screenshots. The end of the video shows the account deletion screen (MY tab > 내 계정 (My account) > 계정 삭제 (Delete account)); nothing was deleted.

3. What can be tested anywhere

  • Sign-up, sign-in and account deletion
  • Video coaching with the sample clip above
  • Starting and stopping a recording (track and elapsed time appear; runs do not, without a slope)
  • Weekly ranking (Resort tab > 주간 랭킹 (Weekly ranking))
